## why norskpiperehabilitering.no is winning

### 1. keyword-in-domain — the biggest single factor

their domain is `norskpiperehabilitering.no`. it contains the exact search term "piperehabilitering" as a substring. for Norwegian local-intent queries, Google still gives substantial weight to EMD (exact match domain) signals — especially when the query is a generic noun with few strong brand competitors. `pipe-rehab.no` has zero keyword signal in the domain.

**fix options:** can't change the domain now without nuking existing link equity. mitigate by doubling down on on-page and entity signals. this is the hardest gap to close.

### 2. physical Skien address in Brreg and Google Business

norsk piperehabilitering as is registered at `Rødmyrjordet 6, 3735 Skien`. that address appears verbatim in their Google snippet. Google cross-references Brreg → Google Business Profile → on-page NAP and gives strong local-pack signals to businesses with a consistent physical address in the target city.

**pipe-rehab.no status:** GBP is claimed (2026-04-14) but the business address needs to be confirmed as Skien-based with a consistent NAP across all directories. our seo.md notes that Norwegian directory presence (proff, 1881, gulesider) is still not done — this is where NAP consistency lives.

### 3. Facebook page appearing as a second organic result

their Facebook page (`Norsk Piperehabiliting AS | Skien`, 270+ followers) ranks as an independent organic result for "piperehabilitering skien". this effectively gives them two of the top three results. Google treats Facebook pages as authoritative for local businesses when they have follower signals, consistent NAP, and engagement.

**pipe-rehab.no status:** no Facebook page. this is a gap that costs us a free second SERP slot.

### 4. multi-page site with dedicated service pages

norskpiperehabilitering.no has a proper multi-page structure:
- `/` — homepage
- `/rehabilitering` — dedicated service page with full content on skorsteinsrehabilitering + kamerainspeksjon
- `/om-oss` — about page with certified-installer credentialing
- `/kontakt-oss` — contact page with structured form + business hours

each page can independently rank and accumulates crawl authority. pipe-rehab.no is a single-page site with 12 city landing pages appended — but no per-service pages at `/piperehabilitering`, `/inspeksjon`, `/foring-stalpipe`, or `/pris`. this is already in seo.md as a known gap.

### 5. certified installers explicitly stated ("sertifiserte montører")

the om-oss page states: *"Vi er et firma bestående av sertifiserte montører som hele tiden fokuserer på kvalitet, sikkerhet og effektivitet."*

this is a significant trust and E-E-A-T signal. Google evaluates expertise signals for trade businesses. pipe-rehab.no currently has no mention of qualifications.

**fix:** add a visible credential statement to the homepage and schema. if the client has any fagbrev, FG-godkjenning, or similar — surface it. even "over X years experience" matters.

### 6. 15-year material warranty

their om-oss page mentions a **15-year material warranty**. this is a conversion-critical differentiation that pipe-rehab.no does not surface anywhere. for a 30-80k NOK job, warranty is a major trust signal.

**fix:** if the client offers any warranty on materials or labour, add it explicitly to the homepage, the contact section, and the schema.

### 7. 12-month interest-free financing

norsk piperehabilitering offers **12 months rentefri avdragsordning** (interest-free payment plan). this removes a significant barrier for high-ticket residential jobs. pipe-rehab.no offers no financing.

**fix:** check with client whether they can offer a financing option (Santander Consumer, Klarna B2B, or other). if yes, surface it prominently. if not, compensate by emphasizing "fast pris" and "skriftlig tilbud" more strongly as risk-reducers.

### 8. Mittanbud profile linked from homepage

norsk piperehabilitering links to a Mittanbud profile from their homepage. pipe-rehab.no's mittanbud setup has not been done per seo.md. mittanbud is described in our marketing-channels doc as the single highest-leverage non-Google channel for this business (20-35% paid conversion rate).

**fix:** create and fully populate the Mittanbud profile. this is in the seo.md backlog but has not shipped.

### 9. Next.js framework (vs. static HTML)

they're running on Next.js. while this doesn't directly affect rankings, it suggests they have a developer or agency behind the site, meaning the technical foundation will likely improve over time. our static-HTML approach is fine for performance but requires more manual effort per update.
