Google Ads UI shows "Can't link ads to your Business Profile" on the campaign overview. This prevents location extensions from showing on ads (address, map, directions).

## what this fixes

Without the GBP link, ads show without location info — competitors with linked profiles get an extra line in the ad showing their address/distance, which improves CTR for local queries.

## how to fix

1. In Google Ads → **Assets** → **Location assets** → **+ Asset** → **Link a Business Profile**
2. Select the Piperehabilitering AS profile
3. If that fails: check GBP ownership — the profile may need to be claimed/verified under the same Google account as the Ads account (`f4investas@gmail.com`)

GBP page: google.com/business — confirm it's claimed and verified under `f4investas@gmail.com`.
